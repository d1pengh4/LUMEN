## DECISIVE GNINA VERDICT — round 2 (2026-07-02): SAFETY STOP (over-optimization)
GNINA-scored round-2 top 15 (`results/phase2b/FINAL_optimized_combined.csv`).

| | Round 1 | Round 2 |
|---|---|---|
| ML pred_pchembl best | 7.34 | **7.78** (higher) |
| **GNINA CNNaffinity best** | **7.06** | **6.64** (LOWER) |

**Key finding (honest, and a real methodology result):** round 2's ML score went
UP (7.34→7.78) but its GNINA score went DOWN (7.06→6.64). The GA over-fit to the
ML proxy — drifting into a region the ML regressor loves but the validated tool
(GNINA) does not. This is classic **reward-hacking / over-optimization of an
imperfect proxy**. More GA rounds are gaming the proxy, not improving true affinity.

**Safety-stop rule (pre-registered in this log) TRIGGERED:**
- GNINA best did NOT improve ≥0.1 over 7.06 (round 2 = 6.64 < 7.16) ✓
- Still < 3 developable/novel candidates at ≥7.0 (have 1) ✓
→ **Declare plateau. STOP optimizing (G2 done).**
