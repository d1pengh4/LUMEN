# Phase 2 PoC (CPU) — fragment-based generation, scoring, ranking

Date: 2026-06-28 · Related: ml-scorer, selectivity-panel
Script: `src/generation/brics_generate.py`
Output: `results/phase2/ranked_candidates.csv`, `diverse_candidates.csv`

## What this is
The first run of the full design loop end-to-end on CPU, as an honest stand-in
for the GPU diffusion model (DiffSBDD). Produces an actual candidate list.

## Method
1. BRICS-decompose the 68 drug-like FKBP12 binders → 78 fragments.
2. Recombine (RDKit BRICSBuild) into novel molecules.
3. Filter: valid, MW 250–500, QED ≥ 0.5, not identical to a known ligand.
4. Score with FKBP12Scorer (binder_prob, pred_pchembl, applicability domain).
5. Rank; also produce a scaffold-diverse shortlist (best per Murcko scaffold).

## Results
- 400 novel drug-like candidates generated; 346 in-domain with binder_prob ≥ 0.5.
- Predicted potency of top candidates ≈ pChEMBL 6.2–6.7 (≈ 0.2–0.6 µM) — modest,
  below the best known binders (the scorer extrapolates conservatively).

## Honest assessment (quantified)
| Metric | Value | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Scaffold diversity | 45 scaffolds / 400 = **11%** | LOW — few cores, decorated variants |
| Internal mean pairwise Tanimoto | 0.53 | somewhat redundant |
| Novelty (median max Tanimoto to known) | 0.46 | OK — meaningfully different from known ligands |

**Verdict:** the pipeline works and yields novel-ish, drug-like, in-domain,
plausibly-binding candidates — but diversity is poor: BRICS mostly decorates a
small number of cores. These are *hypotheses to test*, not drug candidates, and
two known limits apply: (a) the scorer is FKBP-family triage + potency, with NO
selectivity term; (b) candidates are recombinations of known-binder fragments.

## Why this motivates Phase 2-B (GPU)
The 11% scaffold diversity is the concrete, measured reason a real generative
model (DiffSBDD: pocket-conditioned 3D diffusion) is needed — to propose
genuinely new scaffolds shaped to the FKBP12 pocket, rather than recombining
existing ones. B improves candidate *quality/novelty*; it still yields
hypotheses requiring wet-lab validation, not a drug.
