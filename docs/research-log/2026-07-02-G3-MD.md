# G3 — MD pose-stability (detailed record)

Date: 2026-07-02 · Roadmap G3 · Decisive "does the lead actually stay bound?" test.

## Goal & gate
Run short explicit-solvent MD of the top GNINA leads in the FKBP12 pocket; a lead
PASSES if its heavy-atom RMSD stays stable (no unbinding), i.e. mean/max RMSD small
(~<3-4 A) and it does not leave the pocket. This separates real binding from a lucky
docking pose. PASS strengthens the case to hand candidates to a wet lab (GO).

## Leads to test (from FINAL_leads.csv, HQ exhaustiveness-8 GNINA)
- L1 CNNaffinity 7.21: C=C[C@H]1CN(c2cccc(NCC)c2)C(=O)[C@@H]2CCC[C@H]1N2S(=O)(=O)c1ccc(C)cc1
- L2 CNNaffinity 7.04: CC(C)(C)C(=O)C(=O)N1CCCCC1N1CCCC1N1CCC[C@H]1CC(N)=O
- L3 CNNaffinity 6.95: C=C[C@H]1CN(c2cccc(NCC)c2)C(=O)[C@@H]2CCC[C@H]1N2S(=O)(=O)c1cccc(C)c1

## Method
`src/md/colab_md.py` on Colab T4 (detached): PDBFixer protein prep (1FKF chain A);
gnina dock lead (exh 8) for the pocket pose; OpenMM SystemGenerator (ff14SB +
GAFF-2.11 + TIP3P, 0.15 M ions), minimize, ~1 ns NPT (4 fs, HMR); ligand heavy-atom
RMSD vs start (protein-CA aligned) via mdtraj.

## VERDICT — G3 PASS (all 3 leads stable)
All three top leads stay bound over 1 ns (max RMSD ≤ 2.9 Å, well under the ~3–4 Å
unbinding threshold; no pocket exit). This separates real binding from lucky docking
poses for the whole lead set. Combined with the HQ GNINA gate (L1/L2 ≥ 7.0, L3 6.95),
we now have **3 developable + novel leads that are both high-scoring and MD-stable** —
the strongest evidence the pipeline has produced. G4 (retrosynthesis) is unblocked.

Honest limits: 1 ns is short (a stability screen, not free energy); single replica,
single docked pose per lead; OpenCL (CUDA unavailable on this VM). Still a
hypothesis-strengthening result, not measured binding.

Reliability note: a mid-run websocket drop on a ~40 min `colab exec` let Colab
reclaim the whole VM (L2 lost, rerun). Long single execs are fragile; the runs that
completed kept the client connected the whole time (harness-tracked background exec).
